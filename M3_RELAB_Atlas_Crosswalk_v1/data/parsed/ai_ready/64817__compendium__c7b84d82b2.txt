   Introduction
   64817 is a rake sample collected from the rim of a small
    crater at station 4 on Stone Mountain – see section on
   64801.   It is an aluminous basalt with prominent
   plagioclase laths and an age of 3.84 b.y.

   Petrography
   64817 has a subophitic basaltic texture (figure 2).
   Warner et al. (1973) reported the composition of
   pyroxene (figure 3).

   Chemistry
   Allocated to Clive Neal

   Radiogenic age dating
   Norman et al. (2006) determined an age of 3.84 ± 0.02
