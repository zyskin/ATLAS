Petrography
                                                        Keil et al. (1974) and Ryder (1993) give very brief
                                                               descriptions. The fragments are coherent, light colored,
                                                      with minor numbers of vugs. Ryder states that they
                                                          are similar to green-grey breccias (72548 -72549),
                                                       otherwise known to be impact melt breccias. There are
                                                no thin sections and they have never been studied.
