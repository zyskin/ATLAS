Other Studies
   The substantial amount of cosmogenic rare gas that is
    found throughout the Apollo 16 drill core indicates that
    fresh ejecta from North Ray and South Ray craters is
    not a significant component of the core (Bogard and
    Hirsch 1975). Values of 4He/20Ne, 20Ne/36Ar and 40Ar/
    36Ar from the lowest 35 cm of the core (unit A) are
    distinct from all other portions of the core and from all
    Apollo 16 soils except 61220. Heymann et al. (1978)
    also report noble gas measurements for the deep drill.
