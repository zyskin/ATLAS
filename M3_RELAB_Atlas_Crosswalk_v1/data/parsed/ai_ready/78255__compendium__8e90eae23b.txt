  Radiogenic age dating
  The age of this sample is given in the section on 78235.

  Cosmogenic isotopes and exposure ages
   Keith et al. (1974) determined the cosmic ray and solar
   flare induced activity from the large solar flare of
   August 1972 using the top and bottom of this boulder
   (table 2). The bottom of the boulder (78255) was
   shielded from the recent solar flare, but not completely
   from neutrons produced by high energy cosmic rays.
