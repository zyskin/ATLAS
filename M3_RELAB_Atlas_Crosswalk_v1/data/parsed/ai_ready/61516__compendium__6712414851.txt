   Introduction
   61515 - 61519 were collected as part of a rake sample
   from near Plum Crater (figure 2) – see section on 61500.
   They are all friable pieces of regolith breccia from that
    location. Only 61516 has been studied.

   Petrography
   Phinney and Lofgren (1973) cataloged these particles
   and described them as friable, clastic, seriate and “very
    light-grey”. McKay et al. (1986) and Joy et al. (2012)
   reported the maturity index Is/FeO = 0.5 (immature)
   and rare gas content for 61516.

   Warner et al. (1973) termed 61516 a light matrix breccia
    (figure 2), and Phinney et al. (1976) found that it was
   mostly plagioclase with about 35 % porosity.
