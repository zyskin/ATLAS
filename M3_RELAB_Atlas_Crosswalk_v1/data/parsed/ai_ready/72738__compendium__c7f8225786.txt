Introduction
                                                              7273872738 is an impact melt breccia with a microsubophitic
matrix. It has the same chemical composition as other
Apollo 17 impact melt breccias.

Petrography
The matrix of 72738 is microsubophitic, with abundant
plagioclase clasts.  Pyroxenes and olivines range in
composition (figure 3).

matrix. It has the same chemical composition as other

Petrography
The matrix of 72738 is microsubophitic, with abundant
plagioclase clasts.  Pyroxenes and olivines range in
composition (figure 3).

Warner et al. (1978) noted a zircon clast in one of the
thin sections.
