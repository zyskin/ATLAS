   Neal et al. (1994) show a picture of the texture of 12019
   and give mineral analyses.  In an appendix to their
    paper, they describe corroded olivine phenocrysts with
    chromite inclusions that are very magnesian. Some
    olivine phenocrysts are cores to pyroxene phenocrysts
    (<2 mm). Groundmass includes small (0.6 – 0.05 mm)
    laths of plagioclase, pyroxene, ilmenite with minute
    anhedral ulvöspinel, troilite and metal.
