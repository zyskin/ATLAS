   Introduction
   10002 is the number given the bulk soil returned in
  ALSRC 1003. 10084 and 10086 are portions of 10002.
   Please see section on 10084.

   Petrography
   The grain size distribution of 10002 was originally
   reported by LSPET (1969)(figure 1).

   Chemistry
   There are no “bulk” analyses of 10002, as such.
   However, 10084 was a split of 10002, and presumably
   the same (< 1mm).  There is a partial analysis of a
    large split (301 g) by radiation counting in LSPET
   (1969) and O’Kelley et al. (1970)(table 1).
