   Moore et al. (1972) reported 150 ppm C, typical of a

   Other Studies
   Bogard and Nyquist (1972) reported rare gas data for
   14230.   Hoyt  et   al.  (1972)  studied  the
   thermoluminescence of samples from this core (figure
    8). Bhandari et al. (1973) studied the nuclear particle
    tracks as a function of depth (figure 10).

   Processing
   This lunar core tube was cut open lengthwise and the
   core was dissected by Fryxell and Heiken using
   techniqes akin to those of paleontology. Some samples
   from each depth were collected under red light (RL)
   conditions in an attempt to preserve thermolunescence
    (see simplified table).
