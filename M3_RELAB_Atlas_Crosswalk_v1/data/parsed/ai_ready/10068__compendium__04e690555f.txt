   Introduction                                     This sample was the subject of a detailed consortium
   Fruland (1983) describes 10068 as a “coherent,   study led by Abhijit Basu.  It was de-lithified using a
   medium grey regolith breccia.” The original rock was   freeze-thaw technique (Basu et al. 2000), the grain size
   blocky in shape and had rounded surfaces that were   distribution determined (figure 5) and the composition
   covered with micrometeorite craters, although one side   of each size measured (figure 4).
   was relatively flat with no observable microcraters
    (figure 1).                                   Petrography
                                                        Keil et al. (1970) originally determined the mode of
