Introduction
 Lunar soil sample 79120 was collected adjacent to the                           79120
 boulder where 79135 was collected (figure 1). The
  location is on the rim of Van Serg Crater (figure 2).
 The soil from the rake sample, 79510, collected nearby,
 has the same chemical composition.

 Petrography
 The maturity index of 79121 is Is/FeO = 57 (Morris
 1978). A small breccia sample, 79125, was found
 during sieving. The coarse-fine particles, 79124, were
 documented by (Meyer 1973) and are mostly Van Serg
 Breccia like 79135.
