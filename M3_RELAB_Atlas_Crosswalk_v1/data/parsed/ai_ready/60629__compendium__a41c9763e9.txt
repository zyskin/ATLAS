   Introduction
   60629 was found as a rake sample from the regolith
   near the LM.

   Petrography
   Dowty et al. (1974) and Warner et al. (1976) reported
    that 60629 was an anorthosite with attached black glass.
   The anorthosite has a cataclastic texture (figure 2). The
   pyroxenes in 60629 indicate that this sample is ferroan
    anorthosite (figure 3).

   The black glass attached to 60629 was analyzed by
   See et al. (1986) and Morris et al. (1986).
