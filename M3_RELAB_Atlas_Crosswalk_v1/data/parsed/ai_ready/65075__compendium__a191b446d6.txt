   Norman (1980).   It seems clear that the crystalline
   interior is an impact melt rock with highland
   composition (~ 30 % Al2O3).   It has relict ophitic,
   subophitic and poikiolitic textures, but it has been
   highly shocked so that the clast matrix relationship is
   confused (figures 2 and 3). There is a great deal of
    glass. No pyroxene diagram has been published.

    analyses.  Morris et al. (1986) analyzed the glass
    coating for trace elements, but the glass may not be
    representative of the rock. So if someone wants to
    analyze something, please request a representative
    piece of the interior of this sample.
