   Radiogenic age dating
   Borg et al. (1999) determined the Ar/Ar age to be 3.93
     b.y. (figure 6), the Rb/Sr age to be 3.84 b.y. (figure 7),
   and by Sm-Nd isochron 4.3 b.y. (figure 8). Borg et al.
    (2002) and Norman et al. (2003) provided further       Figure 7: Rb/Sr diagram for 62236 and 62237 (Borg

   Norman et al. (2003) obtained a “robust” age of 4.46 ±
    0.04 b.y. for ferroan anorthosites (i.e. the lunar crust).

   Cosmogenic isotopes and exposure ages
    Clark and Keith (1973) determined the cosmic ray
    induced activity of 26Al =119 dpm/kg., 22Na = 53 dpm/
    kg., 54Mn =17 dpm/kg. 56Co = 36 dpm/kg and 46Sc = 5
   dpm/kg.
