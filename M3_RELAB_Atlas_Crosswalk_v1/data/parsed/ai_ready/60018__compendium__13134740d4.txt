--- chemistry block 1; lines 19-31 ---
The glass coating on this boulder is apparently glass
   from South Ray Crater (See et al. 1986).

   Chemistry
    Taylor et al. (1973) and Ebihara et al. (1992) reported
    the chemical composition of 60018, but since it is non
   homogeneous, one does not know what to make of this.
   Ryder and Norman (1980) refer to unpublished
    analyses by Haskin.
