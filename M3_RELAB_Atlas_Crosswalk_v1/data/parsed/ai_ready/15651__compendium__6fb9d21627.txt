   Introduction
   The large rake sample from station 9a, Apollo 15
   included 15651 (figure 1).   It is a medium-grained
    olivine-bearing mare basalt with microgabbroic texture
    (figure 2).  In places the texture has been somewhat
    cataclasized by shock.

   Petrography                                             Figure 2: Photomicrograph of thin section 15651,7
   Dowty et al. (1973) reported the mineral mode and      by C Meyer @ 50x.
   determined the composition of the major minerals
    (figure 3). Pyroxene and olivine are chemically zoned
   and similar to that of olivine-normative basalts. Opaque
   minerals occur in clumps (Nehru et al. 1974).

   Chemistry
   Ryder and Steele (1981) determined the composition,
   finding that 15651 was an olivine-normative basalt
   (although silica was a bit high).

   Processing
   There are three thin section of 15651.
