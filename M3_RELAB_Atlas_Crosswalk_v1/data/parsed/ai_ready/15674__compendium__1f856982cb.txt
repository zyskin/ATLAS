  15676 and 14678, analyzing all the phases.  Ryder
  (1985) provides the only description of 15674, 15675
  and 15683 (no analyses).  The dominate phase,
  pyroxene, occurs as small granular grain enclosed in
  and interstitial to plagioclase laths (subophitic texture).

  clearly reacted with  the  liquid during  later
