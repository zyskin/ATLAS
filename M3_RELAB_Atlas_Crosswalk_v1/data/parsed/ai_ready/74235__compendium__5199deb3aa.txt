(1975) and Neal (2001) have reported chemical

Gibson et al. (1976) reported 2030 ppm S.

Radiogenic age dating
Apollo 17 mare basalts are generally considered 3.72
± 0.04 b.y. old (see Paces et al. 1991).

Cosmogenic isotopes and exposure ages
Eugster et al. (1977) determined the exposure age of
74235 as 175 ± 25 m.y. by the 81Kr method. Eberhardt
et al. (1975) reported 180 m.y.

Other Studies
The rare gas content and isotopic ratios were
determined by Eugster et al. (1977).

O’Hara and Humphries (1975) studied the early
crystallization of armalcolite.

Nyquist et al. (1975) determined the Rb, Sr and Sr87/86.

Nunes et al. (1974) determined U, Th and Pb isotopes.
