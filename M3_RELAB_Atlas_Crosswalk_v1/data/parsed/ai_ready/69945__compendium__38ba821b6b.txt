  Introduction
  69945 is a fine-grained, poikilitic impact melt collected
  from the soil adjacent to boulder 69935/55. It appears
   to be more mafic than most poikilitic Apollo 16 breccias
  and has only minor clasts (figure 2).

  Norman et al. (2006) determined that 69945 was
  3.877 ± 0.011 b.y. old (figure 4).
