Introduction
   67667 was collected as a rake sample from the rim of
   North Ray Crater (see section on 67601). It is coherent
   and has a few micrometeorite craters on the surface
    (figure 1). It has been found to be chemically “pristine”
   and to have an old age (4.2 b.y.).  In mineral and
   chemical composition, it appears unlike other lunar
   samples.

   Petrography
  LSPET (1973) and Steele and Smith (1973) noted that
   67667 was “ultramafic”. Warren and Wasson (1979)
   termed 67667 a feldspathic lherzolite and determined
   the chemical composition. Hansen et al. (1980) also
   termed it a feldspathic lherzolite and provided a full
