   Mineralogy
    Olivine: none

   Pyroxene:  Warren and Wasson (1978) determined
    orthopyroxene was Wo2En63.  Bersch et al. (1991)
    presented precise pyroxene analyses (figure 4).

    Plagioclase:  Warren and Wasson (1978) reported
    plagioclase was An96-97.

    Ilmenite: trace

    Rust: Hunter and Taylor (1981) reported “rust”.
                                                                Figure 3: Photomicrograph of thin section 65325,6.
   Chemistry                                                   Field of view is 1.3 mm. NASA S79-27681.
   Warren and Wasson (1978) and Ebihara et al. (1992)
    determined the composition of 65325 (table 1). Morris
     et al. (1986) found the glass coating was high in KREEP
    (figure 6). Howevr, the interior anorthosite is pristine.
    Cirlin and Housley (1981) determined Cd and Zn
    contents.
