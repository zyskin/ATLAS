 Introduction
 Lunar soils 62280 and 62290 were collected at station
 2 (figures 1 and 2). 62290 is the residue from the bag
  that was used to return rock sample 62295 and may or
 may not be a proper soil sample. The deep footprint
 and abundant fragments in figure 1, indicates the soil
 was soft and freshly deposited.                             62281

 Petrography
 The maturity index for 62281 is high Is/FeO = 76
  (Morris 1978). Heiken et al. (1973), vonEngelhardt et
  al. (1976) and Houck (1982) reported similar
  mineralogic modes for the size range 90-150 microns,
 with agglutinate content about 40% (mature).
  vonEngelhardt also determined the mode as a function            Figure 2: Map of station 2, Apollo 16.
  of grain size. The average grain size is 135 microns
  (figure 5).
