   Cosmogenic isotopes and exposure ages
   Behrmann et al. (1973) and Drozd et al. (1974) reported
   cosmic ray exposure ages of 81Kr = 1.99 and 21Ne = 1.4
    m.y. - which is interpreted to be the age of South Ray
    Crater. The case for this to represent the age of the
    cratering event at South Ray crater is made by Eugster
    (1999).

    Rancitelli et al. (1973) determined the cosmic ray
    induced activity of 22Na = 50 dpm/kg., 26Al = 159 dpm/
    kg. and Fruchter et al. (1981) 53Mn = 135 dpm/kg. for
    69935. Bhandari (1975) determined the activity of 26Al
   = 300 ± 140 dpm/kg. for a surface sample.
