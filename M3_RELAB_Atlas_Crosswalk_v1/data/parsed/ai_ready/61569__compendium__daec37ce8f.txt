Introduction                                Radiogenic age dating
   61569 is a coherent impact melt breccia that was  Norman et al. (2006) determined an Ar/Ar plateau age
    collected as part of a rake sample taken at Plum Crater   of 3.79 ± 0.01 b.y. (figure 4).
   – see section on soil 67500.  It appears to be different
   from most pokilitic melt rocks at Apollo 16 because
    the dominant oikocryst is olivine, rather than pyroxene.

   Petrography
   61569 has a poikilitic texture where blocky plagioclase
    is enclosed in large olivine crystals (figure 2). Simonds
    et al. (1973) give the mode as 68 % plagioclase, 8 %
   pyroxene, 22 % olivine (Fo70-78) and 2% ilmenite. There
    is no rust.

   Chemistry
   Wasson et al. (1977) determined the chemical
   composition of 61569. Note the high Ni, Ir and Au.
