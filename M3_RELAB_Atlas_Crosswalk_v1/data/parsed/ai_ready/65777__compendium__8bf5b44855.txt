--- age block 3; lines 1-27 ---
Radiogenic age dating
    Schaeffer and Schaeffer (1977) determined an Ar/Ar
    age of 3.70 ± 0.02 b.y. for 65777 (figure 8). Norman
     et al. (2006) should have included these samples in
    their study.

   Cosmogenic isotopes and exposure ages
    Schaeffer and Schaeffer (1977) determined an exposure
    age of 8 m.y. for 65777.

   Processing
   There are 3 thin sections of 65777 and two for 65778.
