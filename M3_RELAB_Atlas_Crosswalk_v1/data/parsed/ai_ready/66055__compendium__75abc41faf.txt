    Wilshire and Moore (1973) remarked on the abundant
    glass selvages and glass fragments in 66055. Fruchter
     et al. (1974) were the first to give a report on the clastic
    nature of 66055, and provided the only chemical
    analyses to date. Some of their analyses showed that
   66055 contains abundant KREEP component (similar
    to Apollo 14). Ryder and Blair (1982) also reported
    that 66055 contained KREEP glass.
