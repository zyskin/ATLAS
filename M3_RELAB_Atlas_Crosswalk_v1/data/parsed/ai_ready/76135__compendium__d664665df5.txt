--- material block 1; lines 10-23 ---
Petrography
                                                         Agrell and Reid (in Butler 1973) described 76135 as a
                                                        “vesicular micronorite.”  They noted that  it was
                                                       hollocrystalline and that  it apparently had two
                                                          generations of vesicles (figures 3, 4 and 5).

                                              Meyer (1994) described 76135 as a “vesicular, clast-
                                                           bearing, poikilitic impact melt breccia.”

                                              Chao et al. (1975) state that “76135 may be similar to
                                                   76055”.  However, it is lighter in color and more
                                                               vesicular.
           Figure 4: Copy of thin section photo from Meyer
           1994. Scale is 4 x 5 mm.
