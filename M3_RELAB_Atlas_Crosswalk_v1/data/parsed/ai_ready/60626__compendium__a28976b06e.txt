   Introduction
   60626 is a highly aluminous impact melt rock that was
   collected as a rake sample near the LM.   It has a
    poikilitic texture, but the plagioclase chadocrysts have
   indistinct boundaries.   In appearance and in
   composition, this rake sample is different from other
   impact melt rocks.

   Petrography
   Warner et al. (1976) give the only description.
