   Introduction
   67605 is a relatively large friable, white particle picked
    out of the soil sample from the rim of North Ray Crater
   – see section on 67601.   It is similar to the White
    Boulder samples (67455 etc) – but it is about the size,
    shape and color of a golf ball.
