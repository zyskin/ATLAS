   Introduction
   15014 was collected from bottom of the Soil Mechanics
   Trench (figure 1) near the ALSEP site (station 8).
   Sample 15030 was also collected from the bottom of
   the trench, while 15040 was from the surface.  This
   trench was deep enough (30 cm) for the samples from
   the bottom to be about -10 to -20 deg C before they
   were disturbed (see Keihm et al. 1973).

   The Apollo 15 deep drill core was collected only 10
   meters away – and should contain similar material with
   depth, because there are few craters in the vicinity
   (Swann et al. 1971, 1972).
