   Petrography
   Brown et al. (1975) give the modal mineralogy for
   75015 and found trace olivine (see table). Neal and
    Taylor (1993) described it as a coarse-grained (1-2 mm)
    ophitic basalt with pink pyroxene, plagioclase and
    ilmenite crystals up to 2 mm in length (figure 2). Silica
     is the most abundant accessory mineral.
