   Introduction
   67627 and 67629 are glass-cemented aggregates that were collected as rake samples from the regolith just
    inside the rim of North Ray Crater – see section on 67601 and figure 6. They include numerous white feldspathic
    particles. The exposure age of these glass particles is about that of NRC.

--- material block 1; lines 2-14 ---
Radiogenic age dating
   Borchardt et al. (1986) reported Ar/Ar ages of 1.19
    b.y. for 67627 and 1.41 b.y. for 67629. A clast in 67627
   was 3.63 b.y. old (figure 9).

   Cosmogenic isotopes and exposure ages
   Borchardt et al. (1986) determined the 38Ar exposure
   age of 67627 to be 40 or 44 m.y., and 43 m.y. for 67629.
   This about the age of NRC.
