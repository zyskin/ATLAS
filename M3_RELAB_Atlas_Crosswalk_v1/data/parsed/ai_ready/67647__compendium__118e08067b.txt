                                                 Introduction
                                                67647 was collected as a rake sample from just inside
                                                           the rim of North Ray Crater (NRC) – see section on
                                                    67601.  It is relatively coherent with lots of zap pits
                                                              (figure 1).

                                             Petrography
                                                67647 appears to be a highland regolith breccia with a
                                               low maturity index Is/FeO = 2 (Jerde et al. 1987).
                                                 However, it is broadly similar to other crushed white
                                                       rocks on the rim of NRC. It has glass clasts, and glass
                                                              in the matrix (figure 3).

                                            Chemistry
                                                     Jerde et al. (1987) found that 67647 was highly
                                                  aluminous, with low trace element content, but
                                                               relatively high Ir.
