   Introduction
   61525 - 61529 were collected as part of a rake sample
   from near Plum Crater (figure 2) – see section on 61500.
   As sorted and described by Phinney et al. (1973) they
  may all be the same material. Only 61525 has been
    studied.

   Petrography
   Warner et al. (1973) termed 61525 “glassy breccia”
   and Phinney et al. (1976) termed it “polymict”. Phinney
    et al. (1976) reported lots of glass, but that glass
   “filaments” were lacking.   It is highly fractured.
  McKay et al. (1986) and Joy et al. (2012) reported the
   maturity index Is/FeO = 3 (immature) for 61525.                                                                  Figure 2: Map of station 1, Apollo 16.
