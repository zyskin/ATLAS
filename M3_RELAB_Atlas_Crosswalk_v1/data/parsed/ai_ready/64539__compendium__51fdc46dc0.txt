   Chemistry
   The composition of some of these sample was
    determined by broad beam electron probe analysis by
   McKinley et al. (1983). The trace elements were
    determined for 64546 (table 1) and are quite high.
   Presumably this is for the dark material, because the
    light material is almost all plagioclase. Note the very
    high Ni, Ir and Au content. Thus the dark material is
   an impact melt.
