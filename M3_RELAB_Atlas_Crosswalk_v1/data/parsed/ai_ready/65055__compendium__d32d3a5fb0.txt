Cosmogenic isotopes and exposure ages
Jessberger et al. (1977) reported an 38Ar exposure age
of 2.3 +/- 0.5 m.y. Clark and Keith (1973) determined
the cosmic-ray-induced activity as 22Na =31 dpm/kg,
26Al = 109 dpm/kg, 54Mn = 3 dpm/kg, 56Co = 5 dpm/kg
and 46Sc = 0.9 dpm/kg.
