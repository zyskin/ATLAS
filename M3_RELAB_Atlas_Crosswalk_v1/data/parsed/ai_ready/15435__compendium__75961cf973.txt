Introduction
This is probably the material from the “pedestal” that
15415 was perched on (figure 1).  After 15415 was
picked off the top, the pedestal was broken up and
several pieces collected from the soil (figure 2).  It
was returned in the same bag as 15430 – 15437. This
sample has not been studied.
