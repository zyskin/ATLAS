   Mineralogy
    Olivine: Olivine in 12035 is Fe-rich (Fo64) although
    the first olivine to crystallize should have been Mg-
    rich (Fo80+), indicating that olivine in 12035 re-
    equilibrated sub-solidus. Butler (1973) determined the

   Pyroxene:  Schnetzler and Phillpotts (1971) studied

   and plagioclase in 12035.

    Plagioclase: Walker et al. (1976) found that 12035

    Apollo 12 olivine basalts.
