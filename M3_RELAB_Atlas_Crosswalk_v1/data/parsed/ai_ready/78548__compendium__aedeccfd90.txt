   Introduction
   78548 and 78549 are very friable breccias, that appear
    to be the equivalent of the local soil where they were
    collected. They broke up into fines during processing
    (figures 4 a, b). These particle should be studied in
    direct comparison to the soil (78501).

   Petrography
   Meyer (1994), Keil et al. (1974) and Warner et al.
   (1978) included these particles in their catalogs. Both
   fragments contained small clasts of mare basalt,
    feldspathic breccias and other soil components. 78548
   was found to have pale green glass, while78549
   included agglutinates.
