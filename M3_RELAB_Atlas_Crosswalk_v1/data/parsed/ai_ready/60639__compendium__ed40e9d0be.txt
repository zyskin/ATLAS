--- material block 2; lines 13-34 ---
,39    TS        ,23
                              TS            TS




   Plagioclase: The plagioclase in 60639 mare basalt clast
    is An78-96, that in the anorthosite is An96.

   Chemistry
   Dowty et al. (1974), Murali et al. (1976) and Wolf et
    al. (1979) determined the composition of the basalt clast
    in 60639 (figure 8). Delano (1975) noted the similarity
    to the Luna 16 mare component. The low Ir content
   shows that it was a pristine volcanic liquid. Warren
   and Wasson (1978) studied the anorthosite clast and
   reported analyses indicating it is also meteorite-free.

   Processing
   60639 was chipped, not sawn.
