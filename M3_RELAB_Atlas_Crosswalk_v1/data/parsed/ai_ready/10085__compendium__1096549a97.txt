   The coarse-fine particles from 10085 were widely
    distributed during the first allocations of lunar samples
    (e.g. Chao et al. 1970, Wood et al. 1970, King et al.
   1970, von Englehardt et al. 1970). An interesting aspect
   of lunar samples is that they are fine-grained enough
    for a sample on the order of 100 mg, or 2 mm in size,
    to generally be large enough to represent a “whole
   rock”.

   1227 grains as follows: mafic holocrystalline rock

    fragments from 10085 (table 2) and Beaty et al. (1979)
