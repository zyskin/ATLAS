Introduction
   14317 was picked up at the “North Boulder Field”
    (station H) about 100 meters northwest of the LM
   (Swann et al. 1977)(figure 2).  It is a slabby, angular
   rock (figure 1). One side appears fresher than the rest
   but sides have about the same density of glass-lined
    pits. Several irregular fractures occur at odd angles to
   the long axis. The rock is a coherent breccia with a
   moderate percentage of clasts. Most of the clasts are
    dark.
