   Introduction
   71505 is a “walnut” sieved from the comprehensive
    soil 71500 taken at station 1, Apollo 17.  It is rounded
   and has micrometeorite “zap” pits on 5 out of 6 sides.

    Station 1 was located about 150 meters from Steno
   Crater – which is about 600 meter diameter and thought
    to have sampled to a depth of 120 meters (Wolfe et al.
   1981). There are numerous rake samples from nearby
    (figure 2).
