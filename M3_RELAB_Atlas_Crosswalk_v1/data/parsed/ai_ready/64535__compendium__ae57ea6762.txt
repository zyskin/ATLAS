   Introduction
    64535, 64536 and 64537 were collected as rake samples
   from a blocky area, high up on Stone Mountain, Apollo
   16 – see section on 64501. They are so like each other
    that it can be assumed that they were portions of the
   same rock (figures 1 – 3). However, 64537 has zap
    pits on all surfaces.  64539 – 64558 are additional
    pieces of the same fractured rock.
