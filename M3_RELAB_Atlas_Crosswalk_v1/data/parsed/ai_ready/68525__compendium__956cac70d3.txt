Introduction
    68525, 68526 and 68527 are rake samples collected
   from station 8 soil in an area thought of have
    disturbance from South Ray Crater – see section on
    68501. They appear to be impact melt breccia, with
    poikilitic texture, but have not been studied.

   Compositional variation of Apollo 16 impact-melt
    rocks is discussed by Korotev (1994).
