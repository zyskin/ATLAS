Records show that Larry Nyquist was the consortia

   Petrography
   Phinney et al. (1976) described 14006 as a tough,
    crystalline breccia with 15-20% vugs and vesicles. The
   matrix is fine-grained with intergrown plagioclase,
   cliopyroxene and ilmenite. Carlson and Kramer (1978)
   note that not all thin sections are alike, and that there
    is at least some glass. Simonds et al. (1977) found that
   most clasts in 14006 were mineral clasts (figure 6).

  McKay et al. (1972) studied some of the vapor phase
   deposits in the vugs of 14006.                       C Meyer     14006       same bag

   contingency sample (figure 1).  It is a breccia sample
   of the Fra Mauro type.
