   Introduction
   10073 is a friable, porous fragmental soil breccia, rather
    typical of Apollo 11 regolith breccias. Broken pieces
   soon developed rounded corners (figure 1) and
    considerable fine material (figure 8).

    Fruland (1983) selected 10073 as one of the type
    samples for regolith breccias.

   Petrography
   Dence et al. (1970) and McKay and Morrison (1971)
    described Apollo 11 breccias.  Quaide and Bunch
    (1970) determined the size distribution of grains in
   10073 (figure 6).  Sclar (1970) found a surprisingly

    that the H/He ratio was less.
