    glass spheres in 74220 and 74001 are described by
   Haggerty (1974), Arndt and Engelhardt (1987) and

   and ilmenite (figures 4 and 6) make devitrified orange
    glass appear opaque (black). These black glass droplets
    are found to have the same chemical composition as
    the orange glass (Heiken and McKay 1974). Olivine
    composition is Fo68-82 (Prinz et al. 1973, Taylor and
