--- age block 3; lines 1-17 ---
Radiogenic age dating
  None

   Cosmogenic isotopes and exposure ages
   Drozd et al. (1974) determined exposure ages for 68115
   of 2.08 m.y. by 81Kr, 1.75 m.y. by 21Ne and 2.18 m.y.
   by 38Ar techniques. The case for this to represent the
   age of the cratering event at South Ray crater is made
   by Eugster (1999).

    Rancitelli et al. (1973) determined the cosmic-ray-
   induced activity for 22Na = 38 dpm/kg and 26Al = 83
   dpm/kg.

   Other Studies
   Bogard and Gibson (1975) studied the release of rare
   gases and carbon compounds (figure 10).
