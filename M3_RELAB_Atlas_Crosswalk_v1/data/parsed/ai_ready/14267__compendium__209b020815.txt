   Introduction
   14276 is from the “comprehensive sample” taken near
   the ALSEP station at Apollo 14 (Sutton et al. 1971). It
    is a glass-coated soil breccia (figures 1 and 2).

   14276 was studied by the “European Consortium”
   (Eglinton et al. 1974).

   Petrography
   Phinney et al. (1975) found that 14267 had a frothy
    glass coating on one surface, and micrometeorite pits
   on all sides. The interior is a vitric matrix breccia with
   numerous small light-colored clasts (figure 4). The
   grain size distribution is seriate.
