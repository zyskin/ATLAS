--- chemistry block 1; lines 18-31 ---
Polished thin sections of 76539 show that it has
   numerous tiny phenocrysts of skeletal olivine (figure
   2) and fine needles of ilmenite (seen in reflected light).
   The remainder is “opaque” glass.

   Chemistry
   The chemical composition of 76539 was determined
   by Rhodes et al. (1976). Trace elements were reported
   by Wiesmann and Hubbard (1975).  Nyquist et al.
   (1975) reported Rb, Sr and Sr87/86.
