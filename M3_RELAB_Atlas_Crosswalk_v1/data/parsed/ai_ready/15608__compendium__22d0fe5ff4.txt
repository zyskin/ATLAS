   Petrography
    Ryder (1985) describes this particle as the chilled
    margin of a lava flow. It consists of numerous skeletal
    pigeonite mcirophenocrysts in a finer-grained variolitic
   groundmass of pyroxene, plagioclase and opaque
    minerals (figure 2 and 3). One edge of the particle is a
    beautiful example of “comb layering”. Fine parallel
    crystals of pyroxene project towards the edge.
    Beautiful radial clusters have formed around nucleation
    centers

    There are clusters of olivine crystals in the center.
