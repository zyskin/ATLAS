  Introduction
  Station 8 was supposed to be on a ray from South Ray
  Crater. Soil sample 68120-68124 was collected from
  a “fillet” beside a large boulder (figure 1), directly
  beneath a “spall zone” on the side of the boulder (Horz
   et al. 1972). A double drive tube was collected nearby.

  Petrography
  The maturity index of 68121 is Is/FeO = 61 (Morris
  1978). The mineral mode, agglutinate count and grain
  size distribution have not been determined for this soil.
                                                              Figure 6: Normalized rare-earth-element diagram
  Chemistry                                                       for 68121.
  Compston et al. (1973) determined the major element
  content (figure 4), while Fruchter et al. (1974)
  determined the race element content (figure 6).

Cirlin and Housley (1981) determined 54 - 80 ppb Cd
  and 22.3 ppm Zn.

  Other Studies
  Bhandari et al. (1973) determined the “suntan”
  exposure age of 68121 by measurement of the density
  of fossil nuclear tracks.
