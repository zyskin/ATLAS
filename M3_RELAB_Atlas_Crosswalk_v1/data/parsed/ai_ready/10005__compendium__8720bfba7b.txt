   Introduction
   Lunar drive tube 10005 was driven into the regolith
   about 25 cm, but only collected about 10 cm of
   material (figure 1).

   The drive tubes were pressed into the regolith about
   12 cm and hammered another 12 cm (Costes and
   Mitchell 1970). They were only partially filled,
   because the bit was wider than the tube, which
   required that the dry uncompressible soil had to
   “flow” into the tube ( the design was changed for
    later missions). The weight of 10005 given in the
   catalogs is 65 g (King 1969; Duke and Naugle 1976;
   Lunar Sourcebook). Apparently 15.5 grams fell out
   of the cap when this core was first opened (there is
   confusion about this).
