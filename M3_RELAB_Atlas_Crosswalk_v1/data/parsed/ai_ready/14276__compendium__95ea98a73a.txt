                                                 Introduction
                                                14276 is from the “comprehensive sample” taken near
                                       ALSEP station (see section on 14259).  It is rounded
                                                on all sides by micrometeorite bombardment (figure
      14276                                                  1).  It is a feldspathic basalt that is about 3.9 b.y. old.

                                                   According to Clayton et al. (1972) and Brunfeldt et al.
                                                         (1972), “lunar sample 14276 was part of a consortium
                                                   sample with Professor G.J.Wasserburg as consortium
                                                             leader”.

   Chemistry
   Rose et al. (1972), Brunfeldt (1972) and Ebihara et al.
   (1992) analyzed 14076, finding that it is an aluminous

   remains the possibility that Apollo 14 KREEP basalts
   are recrystallized impact melt rocks and as such are a
   mixture of components.
