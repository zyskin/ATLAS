67617 is a moderately coherent impact melt breccia
                                                                that was collected as a rake sample from the rim of
                                                 North Ray Crater (NRC) – see section on 67601
                                                             (figures 1 and 2).  It has zap pits on one surface.

                                             Petrography
                                             67617 has abundant plagioclase clasts held in a
                                                          recrystallized, now-poikilitic matrix (Ryder and
                                            Norman 1980). Steele and Smith (1973) analyzed the
                                                   pyroxene (figure 4) and the plagioclase An90-97.

                                            Chemistry
                                            None reported

                                              Radiogenic age dating
                                         Nope
