    soil (figure 4) and has been analyzed by numerous
    investigators (table 1). Wanke et al. (1971), Morrison

Chemistry
   12073 has the chemical composition of an Apollo 12
    soil (figure 4) and has been analyzed by numerous
    investigators (table 1). Wanke et al. (1971), Morrison
     et al. (1971), Schnetzler and Philpotts (1971) and Simon
     et al. (1985) all have analyses consistent with the K,
   U, Th of the whole rock (O’Kelley et al. 1971).

   Using a least-squares chemical mixing model, Meyer
     et al. (1971) calculated that 12073 was made up of
    about 45 % local basalt, 6 % anorthosite, 43 % KREEP
   and 1% meteorite. Simon et al. (1985) calculate that
   12073 is approximately 44 % basalt, 6 % anorthosite
   and 47 % KREEP. McKay et al. (1971) calculated 41
  % KREEP. This is a higher KREEP content than most
    Apollo 12 soils.
