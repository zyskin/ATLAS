Introduction
   14265 is from the “comprehensive sample” taken near ALSEP station. A circle 14 meters in diameter was
   drawn and all large rocks were collected and placed in bag 1039 along with soil. It has a relatively high cosmic
   ray induced activity so it must have been at or near the surface.

   Petrography
   Phinney et al. (1975) found 14265 had a glass coating on one side and was pitted by micrometeorites on the
   other side as though the glass coating had eroded away (figure 1).  It may have been a glass bomb.  It is mostly
   matrix with only small clasts.

   14265 has numerous factures, some glass filled. The thin section shows vesicular glass bonding small fragments
   of a polymict breccia (Carlson and Walton 1977).

   Chemistry
   14265 appears to have the same composition as the soil at Apollo 14 (table 1, figure 2).
