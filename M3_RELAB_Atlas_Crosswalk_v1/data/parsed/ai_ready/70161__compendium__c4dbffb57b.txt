                                                Introduction
                                                        Soil sample 70160 was collected from a fillet of
                                                    sediment banked against the east base of a boulder in
                                                          the ALSEP area of Apollo 17 (figures 1 - 3). As such it
                                                     could be material mass wasted off of the boulder or        Figure 1: Photograph of boulder at ALSEP site
       showing location of 70160. AS17-136-20718        thrown next to boulder from craters in the regolith.

  Chemistry
  Rhodes et al. (1974) and Korotev and Kremser (1992)
   have analyzed 70161 (table and figures 2 and 6).  It
   has high Ti, Fe and Sc content (figure 4) and a rare
   earth element pattern like that of other mare soils
   (figure 6).  von Guten et al. (1978) studied the
   distribution of volatile element in different grain size
   separates (figures 8 – 11).

  LSPET (1973) and Moore et al. (1974) reported 150
  ppm carbon (figure 5).
