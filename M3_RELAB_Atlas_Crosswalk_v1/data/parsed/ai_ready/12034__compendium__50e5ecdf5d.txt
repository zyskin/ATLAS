   Cosmogenic isotopes and exposure ages
   O’Kelly et al. (1971) determined the cosmic ray

   dpm/kg and 60Co = 1.5 dpm/kg.  Breccia 12034 was
    shielded from solar cosmic rays, having been buried
    in a trench.

   Basford et al. (1973) determined the isotopic
    composition of Kr and Xe, but do not give an expsoure
