                                                 Introduction
                                              78597 is a vuggy, medium-grained high-Ti basalt
                                                             (figure 1 and 3).  It was collected as part of a rake
                                                   sample and large comprehensive sample at station 8,
                                                    Apollo 17 (figure 2).

                                             Petrography
                                                 78597 is an olivine-microporphritic ilmenite basalt with
                                                            large olivine grains set in a predominantly variolitic
                                              groundmass (figure 5).  Plagioclase laths in the
                                                  groundmass have hollow cores (interfaciculate) and are
                                                     bundled in sheaths with intergrown pyroxene. Ilmenite
           Figure 2: Location where 78597 was found.                                                        occurs in chains. Trace tranquillityite has been reported
                                                    (Warner et al. 1978).
