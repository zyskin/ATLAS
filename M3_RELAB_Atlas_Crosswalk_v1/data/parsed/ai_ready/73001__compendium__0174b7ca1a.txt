   Introduction
   73001 is the bottom segment of a double drive tube,
   from below 22 cm depth. It was placed in a core sample
   vacuum container (CSVC), and as of 2011, it has not
   been opened.  Since it was collected from below 22
   cm, by simply pounding it in, it may have still been
   “frozen” at the time it was sealed (regolith below about
   20 cm is about 250 ± 2 deg K, (Keihm and Langseth
   1973).

   The upper segment, 73002, has also never been opened,
   but X-rays show that it contains several rocks (figure
    4). 73220-80 is a nearby trench, and 73210 is a good
    soil for comparison. Note that in figure 1, the double
   drive tube appears to have been completely filled.
