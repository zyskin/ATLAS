   Introduction
   77537 is a rake sample from station 7, Apollo 17.  It
    appears to be another impact melt rock, but there are
   no thin sections nor other allocations.  It has zap pits
   on all sides (figures 1 – 3).

   Petrography
   Keil et al. (1974) and Meyer (1994) gave brief
    descriptions of 77537. It is coherent without fractures
   and has10-20% vesicles ranging in size from <1 mm
   up to 15 mm. Clasts are difficult to discern.
                                                         77537
