                                           Cosmogenic isotopes and exposure ages
                                                    Clark and Keith (1973) reported the  natural and
                                                cosmic-ray-induced radionuclides for 63335.
                                                Alexander and Kahl (1974) determined an 38Ar
                                                   exposure age of 41 +/- 8 m.y., similar to what is
                                                      expected for North ray Crater, but since these samples
                                                 were from the side of the boulder there must be a
                                                           shielding effect.
