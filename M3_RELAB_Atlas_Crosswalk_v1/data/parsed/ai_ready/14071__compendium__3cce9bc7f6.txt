Introduction
  Sample 14071 was picked up on the flank of Cone Crater (Swann et al. 1971).  It has not been allocated.

  Petrography
  14071 has homogeneous igneous texture (Wilshire and Jackson 1972; Carlson and Walton 1978).
  The only thin section (,2) shows a high proportion of mafic minerals. (olivine and high-Ca
  pyroxene)(figure 2).  It is probably a mare basalt.
