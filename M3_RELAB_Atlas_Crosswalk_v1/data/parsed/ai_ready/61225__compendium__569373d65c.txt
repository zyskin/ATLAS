   Crater (see section on 61221). It has been dated at 3.9

   Petrography
   Norman and Ryder (1980) give a very brief description
   of this particle. It was covered with chalky-white dust
   on most sides. However, it appeared igneous to them.

   Marvin (1972) cataloged 4 – 10 mm coarse-fine
    particles from 61224 (and 61244) and Marvin and
   Mosie (1980) created a catalog of 1 - 4 mm coarse-
    fines from 61222-23 (but not 61242-43). In this process
   they noted a number of white particles, some of which
  may be additional pieces of impact melt similar to
   61225.
