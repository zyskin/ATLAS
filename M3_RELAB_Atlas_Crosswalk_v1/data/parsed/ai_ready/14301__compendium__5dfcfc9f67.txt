Cosmogenic isotopes and exposure ages
   14301 was found to have 26Al activity of 62 dpm/kg,
   22Na = 27 dpm/kg and 56Co = 8 dpm/kg (Keith et al.
   1972).

   Drozd et al. (1974) and Crozaz et al. (1972) determined
   the exposure age of 102 ± 30 m.y. by the 81Kr method
    (figure 12), while Bhandari et al. (1972) reported a
    track age (subdecimeter) of only 8 m.y. Crozaz et al.
   (1972) give a long discussion.
