   Petrography
   71175 has a subophitic texture with long crystals of
   plagioclase (> 2mm) intergrown with pyroxene (figure
    6).  Abundant equigranular ilmenite is sprinkled
   throughout. Large (up to 0.8 mm) regions of silica are
   reported (Neal and Taylor 1993).

   Roedder and Weiblen (1975) studied K-rich silicate
    inclusions in ilmenite. However, pyroxene composition
   has not bee reported.
