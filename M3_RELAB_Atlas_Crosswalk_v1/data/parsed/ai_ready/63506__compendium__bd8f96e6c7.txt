   Introduction
   Lunar soils are sieved and particles larger than 1 cm
   are given numbers – see section on soil 63500. This
   round particle is coherent and appears to be mostly
   plagioclase (figure 1).

   Petrography
   63506 was termed “meta-basalt” by Warner et al.
   (1973). However, it has numerous relic clasts and is
    clearly a breccia (figure 2). According to Warner et al.
   (1973), 63506 is crystalline and similar to 61549.
