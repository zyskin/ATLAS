                                                 Introduction
                                                78575 is a well-rounded granular high-Ti basalt with
                                                low Rb content (figure 1).  It is from a rake sample
                                                            collected as part of a large comprehensive sample at
                                                               station 8, Apollo 17 (figure 2).

                                                                          al. 1978). Pyroxene tends to form in clusters and has
