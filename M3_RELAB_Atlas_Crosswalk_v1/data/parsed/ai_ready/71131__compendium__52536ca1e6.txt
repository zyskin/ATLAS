 Introduction
 Soil samples 71130 and 71150 were collected close to
 a basalt boulder on the rim of a small blocky crater at
 station 1 (figure1). They include numerous basalt
 fragments.

 Petrography
 Morris (1978) determined the maturity index (Is/FeO)
 of 71131 and 71151 as 33 and 34, respectively. The
 small blocky crater is probably young (Wolfe et al.
 1981).
