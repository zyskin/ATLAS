   Petrography
    Steele and Smith (1973) state that “feldspar-rich basalt
   67559 has pyroxene and olivine ranges essentially
    identical to those of 68415”. Ryder and Norman (1980)
   and Vaniman and Papike (1981) noticed the same thing
    (figure 3).  Reimold et al. (1985) separated the
    mesostasis and studied it carefully by SEM.

    Plagioclase grains up to 1 mm are zoned from An98-90.
