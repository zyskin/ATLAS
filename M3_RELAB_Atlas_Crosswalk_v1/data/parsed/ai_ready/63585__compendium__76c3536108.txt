   Introduction
   63585 is an impact melt breccia with regions that have
    basaltic texture and other areas that are poikilitic.  It
   was collected as a rake sample from station 13, on the
    flank of North Ray Crater, Apollo 16, and has numerous
   micrometeorite craters on the surface (figure 1).

   Petrography
   Warner et al. (1973) reported 63585 as a “basalt”, while
   von Engelhardt (1979) found it was a poikilitic impact
    breccia. So it has regions with both textures (figures 2
   and 3). The analyses of mafic mineral in the basaltic
   region are reproduced in figure 4. Hunter and Taylor
   (1980) reported lots of rust in 63585.
