   Chemistry
   The chemical composition of 72701 is similar to other
    samples of the “landslide” or “light mantle” off of the
   South Massif.  It is aluminous and enriched in trace
    elements (figures 3 and 6).  It also has a high content
    of meteoritic siderophile elements (Ni, Ir and Au).
