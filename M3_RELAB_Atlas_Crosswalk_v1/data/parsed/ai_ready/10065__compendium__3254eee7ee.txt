   Chemistry
   Goles et al. (1970), Annell and Heltz (1970), Wiesmann
   and Hubbard (1975) and Rhodes and Blanchard (1981)
   analyzed 10065. The REE are similar to the Apollo 11
    soil (figure 4). Annell et al. found 169 ppm Ni, but the
    critical meteoritic siderophiles (Ir and Au) have never
   been determined.  Thiemens and Clayton (1980)
   determined 104 ppm nitrogen (with a very negative
    delta 15N).
