   Butler (1973), Keil et al. (1974), Warner et al (1978)
   and Meyer (1994) briefly describe 78535 in their
    catalogs.  “The breccia matrix consists of abundant
   small mineral clasts together with dark brown glass

Chemistry
   The chemical analysis of 78535 by Laul and Schmitt
   (1975) seems to be more aluminous than 78546, which
   otherwise seems similar (figure 10), but the REE
   analyses are similar to the local soil (figure 11).

   Processing
   There are a couple thin sections each for 78535,78537
   and 78568.
