 Mineralogy
 Olivine: none

 Pyroxene: Dowty et al. (1974) studied the complex
         zoning in pyroxenes (figure 3).

 Spinel:  Nehru et al. (1974) found that there was a
  distinct transition from chromite cores to ulvospinel
 overgrowth in the spinel grains.

  Silica: Tridymite occurs as large parallel intergrowths
  in the outer margins of pyroxene and plagioclase grains.
  Cristobalite is also present in the interstices.
