 Introduction
 72548 and 72549 are rake samples from the South
 Massif (figure 2). They are described as microgranular
 impact melt breccias by Warner et al. (1978).

 Petrography                                       72548 72548 and 72549 appear to have similar modes, mineral
 compositions (figures 8 and 9) and textures. They are
  clastic in nature but have been partially melted and
  recrystallized (figures 4 - 7). The crystalline matrix
 appears different from most impact melt rocks, but that
 may be unimportant.
