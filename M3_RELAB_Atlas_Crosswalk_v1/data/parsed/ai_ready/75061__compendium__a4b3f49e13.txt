                                                Introduction
                                              75060 was collected from the top surface of a flat
                                                      boulder of basalt on the rim of Camelot Crater.  It is
                                            made up of comminuted pieces of that boulder mixed
                                                    with some soil that was undoubtedly kicked up by
                                                        meteorite impacts in the adjacent soil. 75075 is a large
                                                        piece of the boulder and 75080 is a sample of nearby
                                                                 soil (also very basaltic).

                                             Petrography
                                             The maturity of 75061 is Is/FeO = 33 and the average
                                                         grain size is 112 microns (Morris 1978, Graf 1993).
