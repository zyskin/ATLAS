   Introduction
   15076 is a coarse-grained, porphyritic pigeonite basalt   pyroxferroite.  Plagioclase occurs as hollow straws.
   with subophitic matrix.  It has been dated at 3.35 b.y.  Other minerals are ilmenite, ulvospinel, tridymite,
   and has been exposed on the lunar surface for ~300   cristobalite, troilite and metallic iron. There is residual
   m.y. (Elbow Crater ?). The top surface is rounded with   glass in the interstices (Brown et al. 1972).
