Introduction
                                                67615 is a rake sample from inside the rim of North
                                             Ray Crater (NRC) – see section on 67601. It is unusual
                                                              in that it has olivine as the main mafic mineral and it
                                          may have a high proportion of opaque phases (figure
                                                                  3).  It is rounded and has zap pits on all sides (figure
                                                                  1).

                                             Petrography
                                                67615 has abundant clasts of plagioclase with a matrix
                                                                that is described as micropoikilitic by Steele and Smith
                                                       (1973) and basaltic by Ryder and Norman (1980)(figure
                                                               3).  Steele and Smith (1973) determined that the
                                                        plagioclase was An92-97 and olivine Fo52-64.  67615
                                                         apparently lacks pyroxene (figure 4).
