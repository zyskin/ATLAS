   Petrography
   14051 has the texture of an “impact melt rock”, similar
     to the large boulders at Apollo 17. The fine grain matrix
    has recrystallized with formation of distinct grains of
     poikilitic ilmenite (figure 4). The void space has
    consolidated into distinct, millimeter-sized vugs and
     vesicles. Stadermann et al. (1991) give the mode.
