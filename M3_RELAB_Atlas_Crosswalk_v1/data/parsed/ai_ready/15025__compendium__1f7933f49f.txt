Introduction
   15025 was included in the collection of the contingency
   sample for Apollo 15.  It is a typical soil breccia.

   Petrography
  McKay et al. (1989) reported that the maturity
   index for 15025 was Is/FeO = 30.  It has clasts of
   mare basalt and KREEP basalt, as well as abundant
   glass.

   See also soil sample 15020.

   Chemistry
   15025 has higher trace element content than Apollo
   15 soils (figure 5).  Best and Minkin (1972) studied
   the composition of glass in 15025.
