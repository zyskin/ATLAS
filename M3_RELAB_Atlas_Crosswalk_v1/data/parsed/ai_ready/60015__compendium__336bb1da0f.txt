   Introduction
   60015 is a cataclastic anorthosite that is rather pure in  age of about 2 m.y. (South Ray Crater?) and an Ar/Ar
   bulk composition.   It was collected near the Lunar  age of 3.5 b.y. (although it is probably older). The
   Module but was not “oriented” by surface photography.   interior anorthosite is ferroan and chemically “pristine”
     It has a thick coat of glass splashed over all surfaces  (Ryder and Norman 1980).  The sample was once
   except B1 (bottom). 60015 has a cosmic-ray exposure  termed “Blue Genesis” (Sutton 1981).
