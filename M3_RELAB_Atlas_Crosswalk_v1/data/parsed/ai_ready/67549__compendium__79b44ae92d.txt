   Introduction
   67549 and 67546 are rake samples from the rim of
   North Ray Crater – see section on 67481. They are
   porous and rounded with a light matrix and both light
   and dark clasts.

   Petrography
   67549 has about 35 % low-Ca pyroxene; the rest is
    plagioclase (Ryder and Norman 1980). It also has some
    clasts with light-brown melt (devitrifed?)(figure 4).
