                                            Petrography
                                                    Fruland (1983) and Simon et al. (1990) included 74115
                                                                     is their study of regolith breccia. Ryder (1993) and
                                                 Neal and Taylor (1993) termed these particles “light
                                                    gray breccias”, but they are nothing other than soil
                                                          clods. They are very friable and porous. They contain
                                                  both highland and mare regolith materials, mixed
                                                           together.

--- chemistry block 1; lines 1-13 ---
Figure 4: Rough ride on way to Shorty Crater.


  Chemistry
  Simon et al. (1980) determined the chemical
   composition. The sample has a rather high content of
   Al2O3 – proof that this is light mantle material.

  Processing
  74110 and 74115-74119 were collected and returned
   in the same bag and are probably all the same material.
   There are currently 11 thin sections of these particles.
