   Introduction
   14309 is a grab sample, collected somewhere along the traverse to Cone Crater (location unknown).  It is a
   rounded vitric matrix breccia and has not been studied. There are glass-lined impact pits on the surface (figure
    1).

   Petrography
   Thin sections show the matrix of 14309 is cemented by vesicular glass (figure 2). Chao et al. (19720 and
   Simonds et al. (1977) would consider this a glass-matrix regolith breccia.

   Vaniman et al. (1981) found the glass had the composition of medium KREEP.

   Chemistry
   None

   Processing
   Sample 14309 was returned in weigh bag 1031 with other grab samples from EVA2.
