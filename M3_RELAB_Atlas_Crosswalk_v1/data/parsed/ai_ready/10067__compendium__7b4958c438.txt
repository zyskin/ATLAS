   Introduction                               Chemistry
   10067 is a typical Apollo 11 soil breccia.            The composition of 10067, as reported by Goles et al.
                                                         (1970), seems to have more FeO than Apollo soils
   Petrography                                           (figure 3).
   Carter and McGregger (1970) and Keil et al. (1970)
    briefly mentioned 10067 in their study of Apollo 11  Processing
    regolith breccias.                                Apollo 11 samples were originally described and
                                                        cataloged in 1969 and “recataloged” by Kramer et al.
                                                         (1977). There are 4 thin sections.
