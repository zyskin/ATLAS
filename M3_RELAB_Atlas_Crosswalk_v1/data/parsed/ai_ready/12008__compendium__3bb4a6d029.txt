   Mineralogy
    Olivine: Olivine in 12008 is mostly Fo72 (Dungan and
   Brown 1977).  Butler (1973) determined the minor
    element content of olivine.

   Pyroxene: Dungan and Brown (1977) reported the
    composition of dendrites of pyroxene in 12008 (figure
    3).

    Metal: Brett et al. (1971) determined the Ni content
    of minute metallic iron grains in 12008 (figure 4).
