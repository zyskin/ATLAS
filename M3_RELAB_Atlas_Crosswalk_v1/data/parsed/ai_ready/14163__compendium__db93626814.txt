                                                               transcript indicates that the bulk soil sample was
                                                    scooped from a small (2 foot) crater with glass in the
                                                       bottom, possibly secondary in origin. 14163 should
                                                    be compared with 14259, which is more of a surface
                                                       sample.       14163
