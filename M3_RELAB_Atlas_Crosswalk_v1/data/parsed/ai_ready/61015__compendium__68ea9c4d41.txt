   Introduction
   Breccia 61015 is one of the best examples of the Apollo
   16 dimict breccias (figures 1 and 2). These breccias
    consist of anorthositic material and impact melt rock
   with high Al content.

   Breccia 61015 was collected from on top of the soil
   about 10 m south of Plum Crater.  It was coated with
   black glass on one side and is thought to be ejecta from
                                                                Figure 2: Micrometeorite count on top and side of   South Ray Crater.  Micrometeorite bombardment
                                                         61015 (from Neukum et al. 1973).
