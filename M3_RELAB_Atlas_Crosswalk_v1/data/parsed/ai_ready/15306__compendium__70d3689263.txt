   Introduction
   15306 is a regolith breccia collected as part of a large
    soil (15300) and rake sample (15310) at Spur Crater.
   15315, 15324, 15325 and 15330 are additional large
    soil breccias from the same rake sample, and there are
   many smaller fragments from the same sample (Butler
   1972; Ryder and Sherman 1989). They all contain
   green glass spheres, distinctive of the soil and breccias
   samples at Spur Crater.                                    Figure 2: Photo of 15315. Cube is 1 cm. S72-53914.

   Petrography
   15306 was selected by Fruland (1985) as one of the
    “regolith breccias” for the study by Simon et al. (1986)
   and McKay et al. (1989). The other samples in this
   group show the variability among regolith breccias.
